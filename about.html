About Us

We set out to do one thing: bring proper Greek food to Hull without watering it down.

Our story

The Taste of Greece, Right Here in Hull

The Big Fat Greek was built on a simple idea — that Hull deserved real Greek street food. Not a watered-down version, not a Mediterranean-inspired menu that vaguely nods at Greece. The real thing.

We're not another multipurpose takeaway trying to be everything to everyone. We do Greek food, and we do it properly. Grilled meats, warm flatbreads, fresh fillings, and flavours that actually hit — the kind of food that makes you remember the first bite.

Every dish we serve follows the same standard: fresh ingredients, bold seasoning, and careful preparation. No corner-cutting for speed. No compromises for cost. Just proper Greek food, every single time.

What drives us

What We Stand For

Six principles that go into everything we make.

🇬🇷

Authenticity

Not inspired-by imitations, but genuinely authentic Greek cuisine. We honour the recipes, the techniques, and the flavours that make Greek food what it is.

🔥

Freshness

Everything is prepared fresh daily. We don't take shortcuts. If it's not fresh, it doesn't go out.

💪

Proper Portions

We built this for real satisfaction — not Instagram-portion food that leaves you hungry. When you order from us, you leave full.

🌿

Quality Ingredients

Fresh ingredients, thoughtfully sourced. Bold seasoning. We believe the quality of what goes in is exactly what you taste on the way out.

🤝

Community

Greek dining has always been about gathering, sharing, and eating well together. We bring that spirit to Hull — generous portions, bold flavours, real food.

🏆

No Compromises

We refuse to be the kind of place that cuts corners when it gets busy. The standard stays the same whether it's a quiet Tuesday or a packed Friday night.

Our Promise to You

Every wrap, every box, every salad — made the same way, every time. We're the place you crave halfway through the week because nothing else quite hits the same. That's not an accident. That's what happens when you refuse to compromise on anything.
